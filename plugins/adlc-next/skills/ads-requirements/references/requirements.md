# Requirements method

Shared by `ads-requirements` and `sdlc-solve`. Settle the problem and what
success looks like before choosing a design.

## Capture

- **Problem:** what cannot be done today or what goes wrong, with evidence.
- **Users and stakeholders:** who is affected and who decides.
- **Desired outcome:** what changes for them when this is done.
- **Scope and out of scope.**
- **Constraints:** business rules, compatibility, legal or security
  requirements, deadlines, and budget.
- **Success conditions:** observable, and measurable where possible.
- **Existing behavior that must stay.**
- **Assumptions and open questions,** each with who decides.

Use [the clarification method](../../sdlc-clarify/references/clarify.md) for
anything only the user or a stakeholder can answer.

## Check

- Each requirement states a need, not a design: "customers can see the status
  of their claim" rather than "add a status table".
- Each success condition can be observed or measured by someone else.
- Conflicting requirements are named and given to the user to resolve.
- Items the user did not state, and that change scope or success, are
  confirmed by the user or listed as open.

## Record

Write the requirements as a design record, as described in
[the shared conventions](../../sdlc-solve/references/conventions.md); for
task-sized work the task file is enough. Mark the status: draft, confirmed, or
superseded.

## Done

Problem, outcome, scope, constraints, and success conditions are written;
open questions have owners; and scope-changing items are confirmed or open.
